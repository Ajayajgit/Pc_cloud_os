- 2026-08-22 (Mike directed, Claude applied): **a `feedback` skill and the `feedback/` inbox, plus the Proposal-to-Presentation rename.** Both come out of the 2026-08-21 Mike and Taha discussion, recorded as decisions 15 and 17 and items A14 and A17 in `Internal/POWERCLOUD_OS_ROADMAP.md` section 6a.

  **Feedback capture (A17).** New `.claude/skills/feedback/` with two modes. Capture is deliberately not an intake form: the design constraint is that the people worth hearing from are the ones who hit friction, work around it, and move on, so anything costing two minutes gets skipped by exactly that population. The skill therefore **harvests the session first and confirms second**, asking only the three things it cannot infer (time lost, blocking or not, first-time or every-time). Everything else, including what they were doing, what broke, which stage or script is implicated, and the type, comes out of session context. Expensive items only (real time lost, recurring, or blocking) get a second pass: the diagnostic pair (was it wrong, or right but in the way; did you expect it to act or to ask) and a this-versus-that on named concrete options drawn from the real files, capped at three, always with "not sure, you figure it out" as a respectable answer. **Fix suggestions carry evidence tiers exactly as findings do:** observed behavior is [Verified] from the session, the reporter's suggestion is [Reported], Claude's options are [Inferred]. That separation exists so triage does not later read a tired 4pm guess as a requirement, which is the same anchoring failure B15 warns about in solution research.

  **Storage is one file per entry, not a shared file.** A single `FEEDBACK.md` that eight people append to on a Google shared drive produces conflict copies, so `feedback/` holds one markdown file per item and `FEEDBACK.md` at root is generated over them by new `.claude/scripts/build_feedback_index.py` (same generated-never-hand-edited posture as ENGAGEMENT_LOG.md). Ranking is blocking first, then cost times frequency, computed in the script rather than left to judgement at triage; the index also surfaces areas with more than one open item, since entries that look unrelated often share one cause, and the reporter distribution, since a folder dominated by one name says more about who is not reporting than about what is broken. Triage mode clusters by underlying cause before ranking, and every item leaves the inbox as a roadmap item, a punchlist line, a doctrine change, an immediate fix, or a **logged decline with a reason**, never a silent deletion. Verified against three seeded entries plus a malformed one: ranking, closed-item split, diagnostics roll-up, malformed reporting, and `--check` all behave; seeds removed afterwards.

  **The inbox sits at root, not in `Internal/`.** `Internal/` is access-restricted in Drive, and CLAUDE.md guarantees a consultant without that access runs the process normally, so capture had to work for everyone.

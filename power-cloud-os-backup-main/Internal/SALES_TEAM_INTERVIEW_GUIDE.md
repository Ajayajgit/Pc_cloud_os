## Question blocks

### A. How a deal enters and what you record today

1. Think of your last new deal. Who heard about it first, and how?
2. What did you do in the first 24 hours? Where did you write anything down?
3. Do you record every prospect call? Who runs the recording, and what is the meeting titled?
4. Where does information live today: the Pre-Sales Projects folder, NetSuite, email, your head? Which of those would you say is the real record?
5. At what point does a deal get a NetSuite opportunity? Who enters it, and how long after the first call?

*Feeds: capture bar and stages 0 and 1 of the lifecycle (proposal sections 5 and 6, decision 6); the "no pipeline tracker, about 30% empty folders" finding (section 2); the NetSuite entry the intake skill drafts (section 8).*

### B. Direct versus subcontract, and who the contacts are

1. For your last three deals, how many were direct and how many went through a partner?
2. Which partners are active: Anderson Frank, Playa Tech, Systems Accountants, others?
3. On a subcontract deal, who is the contact at the partner? One person or several (a recruiter, an account lead)? Who is the contact at the end client?
4. Who do we invoice on each partner deal? Does that ever differ from the name on the opportunity?
5. When you create an opportunity today, whose name goes in the Company field: the end client or the partner? Has that been consistent?
6. What do you track on partner deals beyond the contact (placement reference, minimums, monthly hours)? Is anything missing?
7. Would you ever look up or report on "channel"? If so, how would you ask the question?

*Feeds: End Client and Partner Contact fields; class versus a Channel Partner field (section 7 record model, Codex point 10); billing party as project customer (decision 12); the Anderson Frank contact text fields to migrate; channel-dependent project creation [confirm] (section 7).*

### C. Referrals

1. Name the last five people who sent us a lead. Who are they, and which firm are they with?
2. How does a referral reach you: email, text, a call, an event?
3. Where do you note who sent it today? Does anyone check it afterward?
4. Does a referrer expect to hear what happened to the deal? Who tells them?
5. What would a Referral Contact field need to offer for you to fill it in?

*Feeds: Partner, Lead Source and Referral Contact fields (section 7); the intake question "Did anyone refer this? Who, by name?" (section 7); referrer contacts loaded as records in phase 1 (decision 14).*

### D. Proposals and who owns the numbers

1. Walk me through the last proposal. Where did you start, and what did you copy from?
2. Who decides the hours and rates? Who checks them? Who signs off before it goes out?
3. Do you use the MSA and SOW library? Which templates, and do they match what you send?
4. Where do the numbers end up afterward: the proposal only, or also NetSuite (Task 1/2/3, projected total, expected close)?
5. Do you ever create a NetSuite estimate? When?

*Feeds: the proposal skill and LOE checkpoint (sections 4 and 8); projected total at $0 on recent opportunities (section 2, decision 3); estimates out of v1 (decision 4).*

### E. What happens at a win, step by step

Ask for the last win, in order, with who did each step and how long it took.

1. Someone says "we signed." Who hears first? Who do they tell?
2. Who changes the opportunity status? Which statuses do you really use after Closed Won (Awaiting Project, Ready for Kick Off, Ready for Onboarding, Kicked Off)?
3. Who creates the project? Do you press the Create Project button? What do you fill in by hand afterward?
4. What do you need in hand before project creation works? What do you usually have to chase?
5. Who sets up Navan and Uber for the project, and when? What happens if nobody does?
6. Who creates the Asana project? Does anyone check whether one exists?
7. Who creates the email group, and what is it called?
8. Who schedules the kickoff, and who sends the invite?
9. What always goes wrong at this stage?

*Feeds: the readiness gate field list and the rebuilt Create Project suitelet (section 7); the Navan and Uber project lists (sections 2 and 13); the one-page Opportunity to Project procedure; the close-deal skill (section 8); the status oddities (section 2).*

### F. Losses

1. When a deal is lost, what do you do with the folder and the opportunity?
2. Do you record why? Where?
3. Does anyone look at the reasons afterward?

*Feeds: decision 5 (retire the `Lost Dead for now` moves); the loss line in DEAL.md (section 6).*

### G. The Monday pipeline review

1. Who attends, and who runs it? How long does it last?
2. What does the review look at: NetSuite, a list, memory?
3. Who prepares for it today, and how long does that take?
4. Would you rather prep your own book, or have one person prep the whole pipeline?

*Feeds: decision 9 (who runs the prep; the enforcement loop riding the existing meeting); PIPELINE.md flags (section 6).*

### H. Where you work, and license and access

1. Where does your day go: Google Chat, calls, email, NetSuite, a Claude Code session?
2. When did you last open NetSuite, and what for?
3. If a Chat message could log "demo Tuesday, next contact 10/20" on a deal, would you use it? What would stop you?
4. Who needs a NetSuite seat, and who only needs to look something up?
5. Would anyone rather sell with only the folder and let the weekly session carry their NetSuite updates?

*Feeds: the both-surfaces interface and the Paco touch capability (section 9, phase 3b); the license constraint and named users (section 7 Access, decisions 7 and 8); folder-only sellers (open under decision 7).*

### I. The SALES_TO_DELIVERY open items

1. Who creates the Asana project, the email group and the folder today? Is it the same person every time?
2. Does the deal folder start in a shared place, or move to delivery later?
3. Is there an introduction email you reuse? Where is it?
4. Where do signed SOWs live? Does anyone make a redacted copy?
5. When does the MCP-access commitment get into the quote or SOW wording, and who writes it?

*Feeds: the five `[confirm]` items in `SALES_TO_DELIVERY.md`; the proposal section 9 resolutions (folder at the OS root, check-before-create, intro email template, proposal boilerplate).*

### J. Open decisions

Ask each as a plain question. Record the answer and the reason.

1. **Native values (decision 3).** Do you fill in projected total, lead source or the opportunity-to-project link today? Would it bother you if Claude set projected total and expected close when the proposal goes out?
2. **Estimates (decision 4).** Do you need NetSuite estimates for anything? If not, can they stay out of the standard flow?
3. **Losses (decision 5).** Is a status plus a one-line reason enough, with no folder move?
4. **Pipeline review prep (decision 9).** Who should run it, and why?
5. **Cleanup (decision 11).** Do you use the Pre-Sales Projects folders or the MSA and SOW library? Would a read-only legacy folder cause a problem?
6. **Folder-only sellers (decision 7).** See H5. Record each person's answer.

*Feeds: proposal section 10, decisions 3, 4, 5, 7, 9 and 11.*

15. Opportunity titles follow `{Client} - {Engagement Type}`.

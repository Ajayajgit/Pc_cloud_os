# Asana Conventions (interim, until the portal replaces Asana)

> DRAFT built overnight 2026-08-12, pending Mike's review. Scope per the Aug 11 decision: the minimum hygiene the executive-summary and task-intake flows need. Every exec-summary defect at the summit traced to input hygiene: "s*** in, s*** out."

## Per task, always

- **Status set, including "not started."** A blank status reads as "stalled" to the summary and produces false at-risk health. Standardize the Not Started label and use it the moment a task is created.
- **Owner and due date on every active task.** No owner means no accountability line in the summary.
- **Estimate on the task** (from the assessment's LOE) and **hours billed to the specific task**, not the general project bucket.
- **Point person tag**: the client contact for this task, asked for at task kickoff.
- **Deliverables attach to the task.** A file sent by email does not exist as far as the record is concerned; the demand-planning comparison that vanished is the case study.
- **Latest update comment carries next steps** under a literal "Next steps" heading; the summary parses comments, and so do humans.

## Per project

- **Sections mean stages**, used consistently (e.g., Backlog / In progress / With client / Done), so "loaded but unstarted" never reads as "in trouble."
- **One project per engagement**; check before creating (pre-sales usually already made it; link it, never duplicate).
- **Deferred/nice-to-have items live in the project** with a Deferred label rather than dying in someone's notes; they resurface when hours dip.

## Meeting and recording hygiene (feeds the same reports)

- Meeting titles: `{Client} | {topic}`: never a bare "NetSuite work session" twelve weeks running; titles flow into summaries and into recording auto-filing.
- Reschedules happen in the calendar (not verbally), so slip history is real.

## What reads these fields

- **The customer-exec-summary skill**, on demand, for the biweekly client-facing summary.
- **Paco's weekly board update** (`PACO.md` section 6b), every Monday at 10:00 ET, into the project's internal team space: past due with owner and days over, due this week, active tasks untouched for two weeks, and the counts with no owner or no due date, under a header carrying the open and closed totals and the age of the project status update. A gap on this list shows up in front of the whole team the same Monday, which is the point. Completed work and the stage breakdown are deliberately left out of the chat; they belong in the executive summary, which Paco links in a separate message.

## Why bother, if the portal replaces Asana?

Because the exec summary runs on this data **today**, clients see its output **today**, and the portal's work layer will import whatever state exists. Clean data now is the migration plan.
